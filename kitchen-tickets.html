Do you have multiple kitchen areas or stations?
For example, a hot kitchen, a cold kitchen, and a bar. If so, you can separate the tickets by station.

Printing
Should your stations see what the other stations are preparing?
When items from different stations are fired together, each ticket can reference what the other station is working on — so they can time their dishes.
HOT KITCHEN ───────────────── Ribeye steak x2 – medium rare ↳ served with: Caesar salad (cold) Grilled salmon x1
Show other stations' items

What should the kitchen see when a serving is fired?
If your staff fires courses separately (starters first, then mains), the ticket can show just the current serving or also include what's coming up next.

Content
Should the kitchen be notified about order changes?
After a ticket has printed, items might be removed, added, or modified. A correction ticket is printed to notify the kitchen. Select which changes should trigger one.
Removed items
Item taken off the order after being sent.
Added items
New item added to an already-sent order.
Edited comments
A comment changed after being sent.

Controls how related items appear when "Served with" is used on an order.

The reference item prints on both its own printer and the station where the served-with item is prepared.
